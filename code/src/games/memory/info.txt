MEMORY MATCH

Turn over cards by clicking them with the mouse, two at a time, and find matching pairs.
Remember the positions and values of cards you have already seen to avoid wasting turns.
Use the mouse wheel to scroll through larger boards.
In Single Player, find every pair before you run out of lives. 
In Local Vs, take turns and compete to collect the most pairs. 
In Vs AI, challenge an opponent whose ability to remember cards depends on the selected difficulty.
