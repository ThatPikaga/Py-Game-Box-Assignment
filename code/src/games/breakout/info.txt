BREAKOUT

Break the wall of bricks by bouncing the ball off your paddle.
Move the paddle LEFT and RIGHT with the arrow keys, or use A and D.
Keep the ball in play, aim for the remaining bricks,
and collect power-ups when you can. 
Letting the ball fall past your paddle costs a life. 
Clear the bricks and try to beat your previous score. 
The available difficulty settings adjust the challenge.
