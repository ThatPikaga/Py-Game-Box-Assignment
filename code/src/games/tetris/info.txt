TETRIS

Arrange falling blocks, known as tetrominoes, to form complete horizontal lines.
Completed lines disappear and earn points, while gaps make it harder to keep the stack low.
In Single Player, use LEFT/RIGHT to move, DOWN to step a piece down, UP to rotate,
and SPACE to hard-drop it. In two-player mode,
Player 1 uses A/D/S/W and SPACE; Player 2 uses the arrow keys and RIGHT SHIFT to drop. Plan ahead as the pieces fall faster at higher difficulties, and keep the stack from reaching the top.
