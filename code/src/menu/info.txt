PYCADE RETRO ARCADE BOX

Welcome to PyCade!
A collection of classic arcade games & a general hub to play all your python games.
Browse the game covers with the LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys, then press ENTER or E to select a game. 
Follow the on-screen prompts to choose a mode and difficulty where available. 
Press S to open Settings, T to open the Scoreboard, or ESC from the main carousel to exit the application. 
Your player name is collected once per session and reused when you play another game.
I hope you enjoy this small launcher.
