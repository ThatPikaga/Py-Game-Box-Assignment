PONG

Compete in a classic paddle-and-ball game.
Return the ball past your opponent to score.
In Single Player Vs AI, move your paddle with W (Up) and S (Down).
In the local two-player mode, Player 1 uses W/S and Player 2 uses the UP/DOWN arrow keys.
Select a difficulty to change the challenge.
The first player to reach five points wins the match; use Play Again to return
to mode selection and start another match.
